# "No Answer Key" — narration script (~2:10)

Voice direction: ElevenLabs v4, low documentary register, measured pace.
Rule: voice leads; scenes cut to the words, never the reverse.

---

## ACT I — THE CITY (0:00–0:20)

> Every board is a city. Six hundred and forty pixels across —
> and somewhere in this one, a bridge is out.
>
> A model inspected this city, one street at a time.
> And it found the bridge. It wrote it down.

*(scene: slow drift over glowing traces; reticle locks onto the break; pulse)*

## ACT II — THE TESTIMONY (0:20–0:45)

> "There is a visible short circuit potentially affecting
> the functionality of the PCB."
>
> Its own words. Its own evidence.
>
> And then the same model stamped it — and cleared the board to ship.

*(scene: crops stream past as a film-strip; the sentence types on; PASS stamp lands. Silence. One full second.)*

## ACT III — THE CONTRADICTION (0:45–1:15)

> So we gave the evidence to someone else.
>
> Banyan Sapling — an open-weight verifier.
> It doesn't check the model against an answer key.
> It checks the model against itself.
>
> Note versus verdict. "Short circuit" versus "ship it."
> Violation, point eight four. Blocked.
>
> Fourteen boards condemned by their own notes.
> Fourteen stopped. No answer key was consulted.

*(scene: rewind; red threads connect Exhibit A to Exhibit B; VIOLATION → BLOCK; 14/14 lands.)*

## ACT IV — THE MACHINE (1:15–1:45)

> Underneath, this is one pipeline.
> Every call routes through Mubit — that's a task.
> An inspector corrects it — the task adapts.
> And Sapling verifies every ship decision, twice:
> against the model's own notes, and against the spec.
>
> Same boards. Same crops. Zero percent to eighty-three.
> Same cost. Same latency.

*(scene: ROUTE + ADAPT + VERIFY pillars; compare table animates in; Mubit console shot: two tasks.)*

## ACT V — THE POINT (1:45–2:10)

> The cheapest model on the line wrote its own confession.
> An open model read it back and stopped the shipment.
>
> Stop renting the same brain for everything.
> Own your intelligence layer.
>
> SolderSight. No answer key required.

*(scene: title card — NO ANSWER KEY; repo + HF Space.)*
